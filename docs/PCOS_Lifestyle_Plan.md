# PCOS Lifestyle Plan

**Profile:** 30F · PCOS with insulin resistance · anemia (Fe deficiency) · hyperhidrosis · hormonal acne · hair loss  
**Location:** Hyderabad, India (IST) · 3 PM–1 AM night shift  
**Goal:** Sustainable 15 kg loss · strengthen proprioception (ankle) · manage cortisol + anxiety

---

## Daily Nutrition Framework

### Targets (1650 kcal baseline)

- **Protein:** 85–95 g (1.4 g/kg)
- **Carbs:** 160–180 g (low-GI only)
- **Fat:** 50–60 g
- **Iron:** 25–30 mg (anemia treatment)
- **Calcium:** 800–1000 mg (separate from iron by 2+ hrs)

### Core Rules

1. **Never carbs alone** — protein within 30 min of waking
2. **≤40 g carbs per sitting** — spreads glucose load
3. **No tea/coffee within 1 hour of any meal** — tea blocks iron up to 64%, coffee 39%
4. **Pair every iron source with Vitamin C** — ascorbic acid keeps iron soluble 3–4 fold boost
5. **Yogurt at meal-end** — probiotic timing maximizes gut health, calcium absorbed separately
6. **Whole grains/millets only** — ragi, brown rice, jowar, bajra, samai (high Mg + Fe)
7. **Whole fruit, never juice** — fiber preserves satiety, slows glucose spike

## Seed Cycling

**Consistency across 3+ cycles matters more than perfection.**

### Menstruation + Follicular (Days 1–14)
- **1 tbsp ground flaxseed + 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds** with breakfast
- Flax lignans + pumpkin zinc support oestrogen metabolism
- Grind flax weekly, store fridge

### Ovulation + Luteal (Days 15–28)
- **1 tbsp sesame (nuvvulu) + 1 tbsp sunflower seeds** with breakfast
- Sesame + sunflower support progesterone synthesis
- Sunflower adds Vitamin E

### Every phase
- **6–8 soaked almonds** (overnight soak removes phytic acid blocking iron)
- Macadamia or walnuts 2–3 if luteal (extra Omega-3)

## Skincare Routine

### AM (7:00 AM)
1. **Minimalist Oat Extract Cleanser** — gentle massage, warm water, 60 sec
2. Pat dry
3. **Minimalist Vitamin C 10%** — 2–3 drops, **wait 10 min** (stabilize)
4. **Excela Moisturiser** — thin layer
5. **Derma Co SPF 60** — two-finger amount, last step always
6. **Sebamed Lip Defence SPF 30**

### PM — Standard Night (most nights)
1. **Suganda Squalane Cleansing Oil** — makeup days only, 60 sec on dry face
2. **CeraVe SA Smoothing Cleanser** — gentle massage, rinse
3. Pat dry — **wait 5 min, completely dry**
4. **WildOak Ginseng Toner** — pat in, wait 2 min (until bottle finishes, then discontinue)
5. **Minimalist Niacinamide 10% + Zinc** — 2–3 drops, **wait 10 min**
6. **Excela Moisturiser** — thin layer

### PM — Glycolic Night (1–2× per month, bad-skin weeks only)
⚠️ **NEVER combine with Niacinamide, CeraVe SA, WildOak, clay, or Vit C**
1. Cleanse (Oat Extract, not SA)
2. **True Therapy Glycolic 10% Toner** — cotton pad, avoid eyes + active breakouts, **wait 10 min**
3. **COSRX Snail 96 Mucin** — STOP here, no moisturiser tonight
4. Next morning: **SPF 60 non-negotiable**

### PM — Clay Mask (Weekly, Saturday)
1. Cleanse as standard
2. **Minimalist Kaolin Clay 4%** — apply evenly, **10–15 min**, rinse
3. Niacinamide — wait 10 min
4. Excela · No WildOak on clay nights

### Weekly — Scalp Glycolic (separate day from face glycolic)
1. Section dry/slightly damp hair
2. **Glycolic toner on cotton pad** — scalp sections only, not lengths
3. **5–10 min max** → shampoo out → conditioner ends only
