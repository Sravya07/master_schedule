# Fitness & 15 kg Weight Loss · 30-Week Plan

**Profile:** 30F · PCOS (insulin resistance) · hyperhidrosis · anemia · night shift (3 PM–1 AM)  
**Timeline:** Aug 17, 2026 – March 28, 2027  
**Loss rate:** 0.4–0.5 kg/week (PCOS-typical) → 15 kg in ~30 weeks  
**Primary intervention:** Strength training 3×/week (insulin sensitivity > cardio for PCOS)

---

## Training Philosophy

**For PCOS:** Insulin resistance is metabolic, not just caloric. Strength training restores muscle insulin sensitivity **before** fat loss accelerates — this is why we prioritize weights over cardio.

- **Strength = primary intervention** (3×/week floor, non-negotiable)
- **No HIIT** (spikes cortisol, counterproductive with anxiety)
- **Cool/shaded always** (hyperhidrosis is real)
- **Progressive overload over speed** (slower, stronger = safer for joints + ankle recovery)

## Nutrition for Fat Loss

### Deficit Math

- **Maintenance:** ~1850 kcal/day (estimated for 3 PM–1 AM shift)
- **Target deficit:** 200–250 kcal/day (0.4–0.5 kg/week loss)
- **Eating window:** 1650 kcal/day
- **Front-load:** 70% of calories before 8 PM (before shift resumes)

### Strategies
- Protein priority (85–95 g) preserves muscle during loss
- No liquid calories (no chai, alcohol, juice)
- Whole foods only (no ultra-processed)
- Satiety focus: fiber + fat + protein at every meal
- Stall protocol: if 2+ weeks no movement → recheck portions (often 10–15% creep) → reduce carbs slightly OR add 15 min walk

## Cool Environment Protocol

**Hyperhidrosis is managed by environment, not pushed through**

- AC or shaded space (avoid 1–5 PM outdoor heat)
- Cold water bottle at hand (2–3 L across workouts)
- Cotton clothes · moisture-wicking if available
- Lukewarm shower after (never hot) → dries quickly
- Aluminium chloride at night (antiperspirant-strength) if approved by doctor

## Stall Protocol (no movement 2+ weeks)

1. **Check portions:** Review actual intake (often 10–15% creep)
2. **Recheck PCOS rules:** Iron-taking + Vit C timing? No hidden carbs? Coffee away from meals?
3. **Cortisol check:** Sleep 7+ hrs? Anxiety down? Conflict resolved?
4. **Reduce carbs by 20 g** (to ~140 g) **OR add 15 min walk 3×/week**
5. **Don't drop below 1600 kcal** (metabolic damage + deficiency risk)
6. **Reassess at weeks 8, 16, 24** (never mid-month)

## Reverse-Diet Plan (post-March 28)

**Never drop to 1650 kcal indefinitely.** After 30 weeks:
- Add 50 kcal/week (10 weeks → back to ~1850 kcal baseline)
- Prioritize carbs during this phase (replenish glycogen)
- Monitor weight (should plateau, not creep up)
- Strength will improve as fuel increases
