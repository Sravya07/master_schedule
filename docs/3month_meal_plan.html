Morning drink reference — which drink, which cycle day
Assumes a 28-day cycle starting Day 1 of this plan = Day 1 of your period (see note below). Adjust to whatever your tracking app actually shows — the logic below still applies, just shift the day numbers.
Cycle day Phase Drink rule
Day 1–5 Menstruation Ajwain + ginger water — fixed every day of this window (cramp relief takes priority over rotation)
Day 6–16 Follicular + Ovulation Rotates through the 6-drink cycle below, one per day, in order
Day 17–28 Luteal Same 6-drink rotation, EXCEPT every 3rd day in this window is Ashwagandha warm water (in warm soy milk) instead — cortisol support for PMS
The 6-drink rotation (used on all non-menstruation, non-ashwagandha days, repeating in this order):
1. Warm lemon water  →  2. Jeera water  →  3. Methi seed water  →  4. Amla juice 30 ml (diluted)  →  5. Spearmint tea  →  6. Hibiscus tea  →  (repeats)
Note: the rotation position is based on the day's place in the whole 91-day plan, not reset at the start of each cycle — so which of the 6 drinks lands on a given calendar day can shift slightly cycle to cycle. The phase rules (menstruation → ajwain-ginger, luteal → occasional ashwagandha) always take priority over the rotation regardless of where it's up to.
Daily rules — applies to every day in this plan
Milk
Soy milk (unsweetened, fortified) in all oats and porridges. No dairy milk. Yogurt (3 tbsp per meal) is still used — it is not counted as dairy milk.
Ghee
Maximum 1 tsp per day total. Used in lunch tadka only. On ragi ladoo pre-workout days, no ghee is used elsewhere that day. Dinners use ½ tsp cold-pressed groundnut oil instead.
Iron days
Every Wednesday only. Marked 🔴. All other days: iron comes naturally from background foods (dal, eggs, oats, seeds) — no deliberate stacking.
No upma
No oats upma, rava upma, semolina upma, or any upma variety appears anywhere in this plan.
Grain — no millet instead of rice
Lunch/dinner grain is Brown rice ¾ c by default. Millets (ragi sangati, samai rice, bajra khichdi etc.) are never used as a rice substitute. Basmati rice is used only for fried rice / biryani-style specials (Saturdays).
Roti — two types only
Dinner roti alternates between Jowar roti ×2 and Whole wheat chapathi ×2 only. No bajra or other millet rotis anywhere in this plan.
+1 boiled egg daily (now 2 at lunch)
Lunch carries 2 boiled eggs — 1 extra on top of the original request, added after a macro check showed the day running ~20-25g short of the 85-95g protein target.
Sprouts + fruit + nuts — snack, every day
Snack cell always carries the day's sprout (weekly rotation), 1 low-GI fruit, and 6–8 soaked almonds + 3 soaked walnuts. Fruit pool: guava, apple, pear, fresh amla, pomegranate, orange — all low-GI. Fruit changes weekly (not daily like the meals) and is cycle-synced: guava every day during menstruation (highest Vit C, boosts iron absorption when you need it most), rotating through the rest otherwise.
Seed cycling — cycle-based, breakfast
Breakfast carries flaxseed + pumpkin seeds (menstruation/follicular/ovulation, assumed cycle day 1–16) or sesame + sunflower seeds (luteal, day 17–28). Swap the seed on any day to match whatever phase your tracking app actually shows.
Shared curry — less cooking
Lunch and dinner use the SAME dal/curry, cooked once. Lunch = rice + curry + sabzi; dinner = roti + same curry (reused) + salad. ~47% of days use soy chunks (nutrela) instead of dal — much higher protein per gram of carb, which keeps macros in range even with only one curry cooked per day.
Egg cap — 2-3 per day
Total eggs shown in each day's header (e.g. "· 2 eggs"). Never exceeds 3. Soy-chunks days use 2 eggs (soy already covers protein); dal days use 3 (dal is less protein-dense, so the extra egg compensates).
Morning drink — non-negotiable
One drink on waking, every day — see the "Morning drink reference" table above for the exact cycle-day → drink mapping and rotation order.
Variety — no repeat within a month
26 unique breakfast / lunch / dinner combos each (repeat cycle ~26 days, never twice in the same month). Pre-workout snack, iron-day breakfast, and Saturday basmati specials each have 13 unique variants — one per occurrence, zero repeats in 3 months.

How to align this plan with your menstrual cycle

Because PCOS cycles are irregular (35–55+ days), you cannot pre-map phases to fixed calendar dates. Use your tracking app (Clue or Flo) to identify which phase you are in each day, then apply the tweaks below on top of that day's listed meals. The base meals stay the same — these are targeted adjustments only.

Phase 1 — Menstruation · Day 1–5
Blood + iron loss. Energy lower. Cramps. Digestion slows.
Add to your listed meals:
  • Squeeze extra lemon on every iron-containing meal
  • Add amla juice (30 ml) in the morning even on non-iron days
  • Swap the day's dinner grain for ragi roti if possible
  • Replace evening snack fruit with 1 guava (highest Vit C)
  • Drink ajwain + ginger water on waking instead of lemon water
  • Add 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds to breakfast for magnesium (cramp relief)
Avoid:
  • Salty, very spicy food — worsens bloating
  • Caffeine — raises cortisol and worsens cramps
  • Large yogurt portions — calcium competes with iron when you most need it
Phase 2 — Follicular · Day 6–13
Oestrogen rising. Energy + mood improve. Digestion strongest. Best phase for new things.
Add to your listed meals:
  • Switch morning drink to spearmint tea (2 cups/day) — anti-androgenic
  • Seed cycling: add 1 tbsp flaxseed + 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds to oats
  • On lunch days without cruciferous veg, add a small broccoli or cabbage stir-fry
  • Add tofu (80 g) to dinners where it is not already present 2–3×/week
  • Include fermented foods: idli/dosa on B days, extra yogurt 1 tbsp
Best time for:
  • Heavier strength sessions — energy + recovery are at their peak
  • Trying any new meal from the list you have been putting off
Phase 3 — Ovulation · Day 14–16 (approx)
Peak oestrogen. Highest energy + confidence. Short window 1–3 days. May be delayed with PCOS.
Add to your listed meals:
  • Add turmeric + black pepper to every cooked dish this phase
  • Zinc focus: make sure pumpkin seeds and sesame are in today's oats
  • Add pomegranate or jamun (¼ c) to snack if in season
  • Increase raw salad portion at dinner from ½ c to 1 c — digestion is strongest
  • Fibre push: add 1 extra tbsp ground flaxseed to oats — clears excess oestrogen
Avoid:
  • Refined sugar or high-GI foods — can dampen LH surge quality in PCOS
  • Excessive caffeine — may interfere with LH surge timing
Phase 4 — Luteal · Day 17 onwards
Progesterone rises. Energy dips mid-phase. PMS: bloating, cravings, anxiety, mood swings. Longest phase.
Add to your listed meals:
  • Seed cycling switch: 1 tbsp sesame + 1 tbsp sunflower seeds in oats instead of flax + pumpkin
  • Switch morning drink to ashwagandha warm water (¼ tsp KSM-66 in soy milk)
  • Add extra protein to breakfast: 2 eggs instead of 1 on A pre-workout days
  • Increase magnesium: add 1 extra tbsp pumpkin seeds to the listed snack
  • B6 focus: make sure eggs are in at least 2 meals daily (B6 for PMS mood)
Reduce in this phase:
  • Salt in all dishes — worsens bloating dramatically
  • Total carbs slightly — insulin resistance peaks in luteal phase for PCOS
  • Sugar cravings: replace with ragi ladoo or dates + nuts, not sweets
